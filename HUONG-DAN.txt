=============================================
  AUTO-LIKE-CMT - HUONG DAN SU DUNG
=============================================

BUOC 1: Cai dat (chi can lam 1 lan)
------------------------------------
- Keo AutoLikeCmt.app vao thu muc Applications
- Mo app lan dau - Terminal se tu dong cai dat
- Cho cai dat xong (5-10 phut, can internet)

BUOC 2: Nhap account
---------------------
- Mo Terminal goi: open ~/.auto-like-cmt/
- Sua file: local_pool_data.json
- Nhap account theo format:
  {
    "accounts": [
      {
        "id": "123456",
        "username": "tenuser",
        "login_user": "tenuser",
        "password": "matkhau",
        "email": "",
        "mail_pass": "",
        "totp_secret": "",
        "cookie": "cookie_string_day_du",
        "status": "live"
      }
    ]
  }

BUOC 3: Chay app
-----------------
- Mo AutoLikeCmt tu Applications
- App se mo cua so giao dien

YEU CAU HE THONG:
- macOS (Apple Silicon hoac Intel)
- Google Chrome da cai dat
- Ket noi internet

LUU Y:
- Lan dau chay, macOS co the hoi
  "cho phep chay khong?" -> Nhan "Open" hoac vao
  System Settings > Privacy & Security > cho phep
- Data luu tai: ~/.auto-like-cmt/
